About Melanie Martinez

Melanie Martinez is an American singer-songwriter, visual artist, and director known for her conceptual albums Cry Baby, K-12, and PORTALS. Her unique aesthetic blends vintage pastel fairytale visuals with dark, thought-provoking pop storytelling.

✨ Concept Aesthetic

Each era in Melanie's discography represents a transformation of her character persona—from childhood innocence to surreal creature rebirth.
